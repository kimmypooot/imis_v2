# NOSI and NOSA — plan and decisions

**Date:** 2026-11-26. **Status:** built the same day. What exists is
described in `docs/architecture.md`, "Human Resources: NOSA and NOSI". This
file keeps the research and the decisions behind it.

## Problem
HRD typed each Notice of Salary Adjustment (NOSA) and Notice of Step
Increment (NOSI) in Word, then emailed each one to the employee
separately. With a whole-office NOSA every January (EO 64's 4th tranche
takes effect 1 January 2027) and NOSIs through the year, this took a lot
of time. IMIS already holds every figure: the plantilla, the salary
schedules, names and leave without pay.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-26)
| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signatory | The **Regional Director** (Director IV). The alternate is the **Director III**. |
| 2 | Formats | HRD's `NOSA_Template_2026.docx` and `NOSI_Template_2026.docx`. |
| 3 | Merit step increments | Granted, rarely. **Not built**; prepare by hand for now. |
| 4 | Signature | Wet ink or PNPKI, both outside IMIS. The notice **leaves the signature space blank** (the Regional Director's preference). |
| 5 | LWOP rule | Unknown. IMIS moves the date by **all** days without pay once they pass 15, shows the working, and HR confirms the date. |
| 6 | 2026 NOSAs | Unknown. Start with 2027. Older paper notices go to the 201 File as uploads. |
| 7 | Payroll differential | Unknown. Out of scope. |
| 8 | Directors | Their notices are issued by the **Central Office**: left out. |
| 9 | Delivery | **No email.** Generate, sign, then upload to the 201 File. The employee sees it under My 201 File. |

## Policy read for this
- **NBC No. 601 (22 Jan 2026),** the 3rd tranche of EO 64. Read in full
  from the DBM copy.
  - §5.1 salary rules: the same grade and step at the new rate; between
    steps go to the next higher step; above step 8 go to the new step 8,
    or no increase.
  - §7: the HRMO prepares on Annex B-1/B-2, the Head of Agency approves,
    copy to GSIS, post-audit.
- **CSC-DBM JC No. 1, s. 2012:**
  - one step for every 3 years of continuous satisfactory service in the
    present position, up to step 8;
  - LWOP up to 15 days in total doesn't interrupt the count;
  - an unsatisfactory rating or an administrative penalty forfeits the
    period;
  - merit increments need a CSC-approved SPMS, take effect 1 January,
    and are capped at 5%.
  - The full text wasn't reachable (the CSC forum was down); the points
    above come from secondary sources.
- **DBM Circular Letter No. 2024-7 (27 Feb 2024):**
  - NOSI/NOSA may be signed digitally, but only with **PNPKI**;
  - a printout of a digital original is a duplicate and should say so;
  - the agency issues internal rules first.
- **NBC No. 600** covers military and uniformed personnel, so it doesn't
  apply.

## The templates (what the print page reproduces)
- **Both:** A4, on the RO VIII letterhead (full-page header image: banner,
  CSC logo, "CSC REGIONAL OFFICE VIII", Lingkod Bayani footer). Aptos
  12 pt, 1-inch margins.
- **NOSA:**
  - The Annex B-1 wording: "Pursuant to National Budget Circular No. __
    s. __ dated __, implementing Executive Order No. 64 s. 2024 dated 02
    August 2024, …".
  - Items 1–3 with ₱ amounts; dates written "01 January 2025".
  - The name without an honorific; office line "Regional Office VIII";
    "Sir/Ma'am:".
  - Footer: Position Title / Salary Grade / Item No., FY __ PSI and/or
    Plantilla of Personnel / Copy Furnished: GSIS.
  - **Two pages:** *Employee Copy* and *OHRMD/HRD Copy*.
- **NOSI:**
  - "Notice of Step Increment Due to Length of Service".
  - The name as on the account (with honorific); office line "CSC
    Regional Office VIII (CSC- RO VIII)"; "Dear Mr. Casinillo:".
  - The JC 1 s. 2012 / JR No. 4 s. 2009 paragraph with the position in
    capitals.
  - Items 1–3 with "P" amounts; dates written "January 3, 2025".
  - Footer: Item No. / FY __ Plantilla of Personnel / CF: GSIS Tacloban
    Branch.
- **Keep out of git:** the templates and the two scanned samples (`NOSA_Uy,
  Rey Albert .pdf`, `NOSI_Aguillon, Otelia Fe.pdf`) contain real names and
  salaries. They are in `.gitignore`.

## Not built (possible next steps)
- Merit step increments (Annex A), when HRD has a template.
- NOSAs for salaries set by hand between steps (§5.1.2): listed as left
  out, prepared by hand.
- Payroll salary differentials when a schedule is entered after payroll
  months are finalized.
- A printout note for PNPKI originals ("The original of this NOSA is in
  digital format", CL 2024-7), if HRD starts signing digitally.
